20' & 40' C-Can Rentals

Need secure storage right on your own property? Our steel shipping containers (C-Cans) are weatherproof, lockable and built to handle Alberta weather, ideal for job sites, renovations, moves, farms and businesses.

20' C-Can

20' L × 8' W × 8'6" H  ·  approx. 1,170 cu ft

A compact container that fits on most driveways and yards. Great for renovations, decluttering or storing the contents of a 1–2 bedroom home.

  • Home renovations & moves
  • Tools, furniture & seasonal items
  • Small job sites

40' C-Can

40' L × 8' W × 8'6" H  ·  approx. 2,390 cu ft

Double the space for bigger jobs. Holds the contents of a 3–4 bedroom home, vehicles, or large equipment and business inventory.

  • Full household storage
  • Farm & construction equipment
  • Business inventory & supplies
